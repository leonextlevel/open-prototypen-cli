# Explore

Discuss the user's problem, audience, constraints, and possible approaches. Ask questions that change the product decision; state assumptions when answers are unavailable. Do not force an artifact from an exploratory conversation. When the idea is coherent enough to preserve, get `open-prototypen instructions brief --json`, write the brief in the user's language, and validate it. Keep structural headings in English.
