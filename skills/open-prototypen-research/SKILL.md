# Research

Choose the research artifact that resolves a real decision. Read its CLI instructions and dependencies first. Seek current primary sources when possible; record URLs, access dates, observations, and implications. Distinguish facts, claims, interpretations, and assumptions. Do not present invented users, revenue, market size, or conversion metrics as evidence.

For visual research, look for references relevant to this product's audience, content, and task. Describe concrete choices in hierarchy, typography, color roles, spacing, alignment, components, states, and icons. For each reference, explain which principle transfers to this product and what would be inappropriate to copy. Record gaps or uncertain observations. The reference is evidence for a design decision, not a palette or layout to reproduce. Validate each completed artifact.
